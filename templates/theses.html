What these are

Thesis Maps are factual maps of supply-chain and spending relationships — educational and for market awareness only, not investment advice and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. The exposure and cycle-stage bands are qualitative judgements from filings and earnings calls; they are meant to be examined and argued with, not taken as precise figures.
