What these are

Thesis Maps are factual maps of supply-chain and spending relationships — our research opinion, for information, education and market awareness only, not investment advice, not a personal recommendation, not FCA-authorised, and not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. The exposure and cycle-stage bands are qualitative judgements from filings and earnings calls; they are meant to be examined and argued with, not taken as precise figures. Capital at risk; past performance is not a guide to future results. Verify against primary filings and do your own research.
