Questions

Things worth asking.

Is AlphaHunt giving me investment advice?
No. AlphaHunt is a research and tracking tool. Every score, pillar, and exit is observational — it tells you what our model sees, not what to do with your money. Use it alongside your own judgment.
How often does the data refresh?
Prices and indicators refresh every 5 minutes during US market hours. Fundamentals (revenue, margins, EPS) refresh nightly. Closed trades and the Top Hunts portfolio refresh in real time as exits fire.
What's the difference between Alpha Score and the six pillars?
Alpha Score is a composite — the headline number. The six pillars (Momentum, Growth, Quality, Valuation, Sentiment, Growth Potential) are the inputs. A stock can score 80 because momentum is screaming, while valuation is mediocre — the bars show exactly which.
How is my data protected?
All traffic is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We never sell or share user data; authentication runs through Supabase with industry-standard JWT signing. The product is SOC-2-inspired in its controls and we're working toward formal certification.
Why does Top Hunts sometimes have fewer than 12 picks?
By design. We only fill a slot when the market actually offers a Grade-A name with ≥75 Alpha and ≥4 strong pillars. In thin or sideways markets the list can drop to 8 or 6 picks — empty slots are honest signal that quality is scarce, not a reason to lower the bar.
Is it free?
Yes — fully free during beta. No credit card required, no usage limits. When we move out of beta there will be a paid tier, and existing beta users get a long discount window.
