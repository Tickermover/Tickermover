How it works

Three steps. No mysticism.

A disciplined process you can see end-to-end — from score to entry to exit.

01

We score 540+ US stocks daily

Every name on the S&P 500, Nasdaq-100 and Dow gets an Alpha Score across six investment pillars: momentum, growth, quality, valuation, sentiment, and growth potential.

02

Only Grade-A names enter the tracker

Our Top Hunts list holds at most 12 picks — and only stocks scoring 75+ with at least 4 strong pillars get in. Empty slots beat weak picks.

03

Disciplined exits, recorded forever

Trail stops, +100% take-profit targets, and signal exits decide when a pick leaves. Every exit is logged so the track record is auditable, not anecdotal.

Methodology

Six pillars. One score.

Alpha Score is a composite. Hover any pick on the dashboard to see all six bars — so you know exactly why a name is on the list.

↗

Momentum

Price action over 1- and 3-month windows, vs. its own 50-day moving average.

⤴

Growth

Revenue and EPS year-over-year. Penalties for declining top-line, bonuses for >20% growth.

◆

Quality

Gross and FCF margins, leverage. High-margin businesses with clean balance sheets score top.

⚖

Valuation

PEG and P/E vs. growth, plus analyst-implied upside. Avoids names priced past perfection.

★

Sentiment

Strong-buy ratio, insider net buys, mention velocity. Captures professional + crowd signal.

⚡

Growth Potential

Forward EPS revision velocity and addressable-market tailwinds. The 'tomorrow' pillar.

Questions

Things worth asking.

Is AlphaHunt giving me investment advice?
No. AlphaHunt is a research and tracking tool. Every score, pillar, and exit is observational — it tells you what our model sees, not what to do with your money. Use it alongside your own judgment.
How often does the data refresh?
Prices and indicators refresh every 5 minutes during US market hours. Fundamentals (revenue, margins, EPS) refresh nightly. Closed-trades and the Top Hunts portfolio refresh in real-time as exits fire.
What's the difference between Alpha Score and the six pillars?
Alpha Score is a composite — the headline number. The six pillars (Momentum, Growth, Quality, Valuation, Sentiment, Growth Potential) are the inputs. A stock can score 80 because momentum is screaming, while valuation is mediocre — the bars show exactly which.
Why does Top Hunts sometimes have fewer than 12 picks?
By design. We only fill a slot when the market actually offers a Grade-A name with ≥75 Alpha and ≥4 strong pillars. In thin or sideways markets the list can drop to 8 or 6 picks — empty slots are honest signal that quality is scarce, not a reason to lower the bar.
Is it free?
Yes — fully free during beta. No credit card required, no usage limits. When we move out of beta there will be a paid tier, and existing beta users get a long discount window.
