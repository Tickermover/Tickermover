📖 How to Use AlphaHunt

📐 Alpha Score

Composite 0–100 score combining momentum (40%), social velocity (20%), technical signals (20%), fundamentals (10%), and market cap fit (10%). Scores above 70 indicate strong setups. Only $1B+ market cap stocks qualify.

🎯 Grades & Ratings

A → ★★★★★ Strong Outperform · B → ★★★★ Outperform · C → ★★★ Neutral · D → ★★ Lagging · F → ★ Avoid. The Hot List shows only ★★★★★ Strong Outperform (grade A) with confidence ≥ 75%. Use All Stocks tab to browse all grades. Note: these are quality descriptors, not buy/sell recommendations — AlphaHunt is a research tool, not a SEBI-registered advisor.

⚡ Trade Setups

ATR-based position sizing: Entry at current price, Stop = Entry − 1.5×ATR, Target = Entry + 3.0×ATR. Shares = (Account × Risk%) ÷ ATR. Default 1% risk per trade. Adjust using the sizer widget.

🔄 Data Refresh

Live prices update every 30s from /api/quotes/batch. Fast refresh (quotes + social) every 5 min. Slow refresh (candles, fundamentals, insider) every 60 min starting 90s after boot.

⭐ Watchlist

Save any ticker you want to keep an eye on. Saved on your browser via localStorage. Tap the ★ icon on any stock card to add or remove, or type a ticker directly in the Watchlist tab.

💼 Allocator

Shows share count as primary metric, position value, % of account, entry/stop/target levels, and max risk per position. Based on ATR sizing. Max 8 open positions, 6% total portfolio risk.
