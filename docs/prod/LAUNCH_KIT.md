We're free during beta and looking for users who actually trade and will tell us where the verdicts get it wrong.

## 10 · Reddit — be useful, then mention

**The rule:** every subreddit below has explicit anti-promotion rules. Posting "check out my new product" gets you banned. The path that works: build comment karma by genuinely answering questions, then occasionally — when AlphaHunt is *truly* the best answer — mention it with a one-line context ("I built a tool for exactly this; happy to share if useful").

**Subreddits worth seeding (in priority order):**
1. **r/IndianStreetBets** — your highest-fit audience. Indian retail trading US.
2. **r/IndiaInvestments** — more conservative, longer-form, but same audience.
3. **r/stocks** — broad US retail. Highly moderated. Comment-only at first.
4. **r/SecurityAnalysis** — value-investing crowd. Reverse DCF lands well here.
5. **r/ValueInvesting** — same. Mention DCF, not "Pop Score".
6. **r/investing** — biggest audience but heavily moderated. Last priority.

**Comment template — when someone asks "how do I evaluate [stock]":**
> Quick framework I use: revenue growth YoY, gross margin trend, free cash flow trajectory, valuation vs growth (PEG or Reverse DCF, not raw P/E), balance sheet (net debt / EBITDA), 1- and 3-month relative strength vs sector, and your own one-sentence thesis on why this company will be bigger in 5 years.
>
> For [TICKER] specifically, what jumps out: [2-3 specific numbers — actually look them up]. The one thing that would change my view is [specific catalyst].
>
> Disclosure: I built a tool that runs this checklist on 200 US stocks every 5 min — alphahunt.in/stocks/[TICKER] — but the framework is what matters, the tool is just automation.

**Comment template — Reverse DCF threads (very high-fit):**
> A useful flip is: instead of guessing growth, take today's market cap and solve for the revenue CAGR the price is already pricing in. If it's higher than the company has ever delivered, you're betting on a miracle. If it's lower than its historical average, you're betting on mean reversion. Either is a real bet, but at least you know which one you're making.
>
> Worked example: [TICKER] at $X market cap, hold the discount rate / terminal multiple constant, the implied 10-year CAGR is roughly Y%. Their best 10-year stretch was Z%. Decide.
>
> (I built a free tool that does this on every stock if useful — alphahunt.in. Mods, please remove if it counts as promotion.)

**Don't:**
- Post a "Show HN" / "Show Reddit" thread unless your karma is real
- Drop the link in every comment — once per thread, only when relevant
- Fake disclose. Always say "I built it"
